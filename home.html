Timber Knowledge

Wood Species We Handcraft

We source only high-grade, naturally aged and kiln-seasoned hardwood logs. Here is how our primary timbers compare.

Indian Sheesham (Rosewood)

Dalbergia sissoo • Best Value Hardwood

Deep rich grain with distinct dark and light contrast patterns. Exceptionally dense, heavy, and resistant to decay. Perfect for heirloom furniture.

Hardness & Strength ★★★★★
Termite Resistance ★★★★☆
Best For Dining Sets, Beds, Mandir

CP Teak Wood (Sagwan)

Tectona grandis • Premium Hardwood

The gold standard of furniture timber. Packed with natural oils that naturally repel termites, pests, and moisture. Beautiful golden-brown grain.

Hardness & Strength ★★★★★
Termite Resistance ★★★★★ (Highest)
Best For Beds, Sofas, Heavy Doors

American Walnut

Juglans nigra • Luxury Dark Timber

Luxurious dark chocolate tone with swirling live-edge grain character. Prized worldwide by architects and interior designers for modern luxury spaces.

Hardness & Strength ★★★★☆
Termite Resistance ★★★★☆
Best For Live Edge Tables, Study Desks

White Oak

Quercus alba • Modern Scandinavian

Modern light honey tones with prominent straight grain and medullary ray flecks. Extremely wear-resistant and ideal for minimalist Nordic aesthetics.

Hardness & Strength ★★★★★
Termite Resistance ★★★★☆
Best For Minimalist Chairs, Coffee Tables
Artisanal Journey

How We Build Your Furniture

From a simple sketch to a museum-grade finished wooden piece in your living room.

1

Design & Budget Plan

Share room photos or sketches. We finalize custom dimensions, wood species, and ergonomic specs according to your price.

2

Timber Seasoning

We select dense hardwood logs and kiln-dry them to ensure zero internal moisture or warping risks.

3

Precision Joinery

Artisans cut traditional mortise-and-tenon and dovetail interlocking joints for unbreakable strength.

4

Multi-Coat Finishing

Multi-step hand sanding, grain filling, stain application, and 3-coat Italian PU or natural wax sealant.

5

White Glove Setup

Carefully blanket-wrapped, delivered to your room, assembled, and inspected for perfection.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our custom woodworking process, guarantees, and pricing.

Can you build furniture matching my specific budget?
Yes! Because we are a direct factory workshop, we can alter wood thickness, internal framing materials (Solid Sheesham, Marine Ply, Teak Veneer, or Hardwood), and finishes so you get the exact design you want at your comfortable price point.
Why are your workshop prices lower than retail furniture stores?
Retail showrooms have huge real estate rents, air conditioning bills, and dealer commissions of 30% to 50%. When you buy from WoodCraft Workshop, you pay only for pure timber, craftsmanship labor, and polish.
How do you guarantee protection against termites and wood borer?
All our timber logs undergo kiln seasoning (reducing moisture below 12%) followed by pressure-impregnated anti-termite and borer chemical treatment. We provide a 10-Year written structural & anti-termite warranty with every piece.
Can I visit the workshop to see the wood and ongoing work?
Yes, absolutely! We encourage customers to visit our Jaipur workshop to inspect raw logs, feel timber samples, and watch master carpenters carve joints. We also offer live video calls for clients across India.
