The 4 Pillars of Our Craft

How we ensure your furniture never shrinks, warps, or attracts pests.

Kiln Seasoning & Moisture Control

All timber logs are baked in temperature-controlled kilns until internal moisture is regulated to 8–12%. This permanently eliminates cracking and seasonal shrinkage.

Vacuum Pressure Anti-Termite Treatment

Timber is immersed in certified non-toxic organic borer-repellent solutions under vacuum pressure, allowing deep core penetration and lifetime insect resistance.

Mortise & Tenon Joinery

We avoid weak plastic dowels. Our frames utilize solid inter-locking wood joints reinforced with waterproof cross-linked PVA adhesives.

Italian Multi-Coat PU Polish

Every piece receives multiple coats of sanding sealer and Italian polyurethane (PU) or natural oil, creating a heat-resistant, water-repellent satin luster.
